## 1.0.0-rc.14

### Added
- **"On this day" widget**: A new home screen widget (small and medium, unlocked home screen only) shows your photos from past years on the current date, in the style of Apple Photos. The photo changes every 30 minutes and the medium widget shows three photos. Tapping a photo opens it in the app. On iOS 17 and later, a button shows the next memory and a cross removes a photo from the widget after a confirmation. The widget never sees your credentials: the app prepares the memories (when it comes to the foreground and in the background, only while the widget is on a screen) and the widget only reads them. The photos are erased on sign out, when the credentials are revoked and on remote wipe. Only Nextcloud photos are included for now.
- **Remote wipe support**: The app now supports Nextcloud's remote wipe (Settings → Security → Devices & sessions → "Wipe device"). When a wipe is requested, the app detects it at startup, when returning to the foreground, or when the server rejects the credentials, erases all local data (offline snapshots, cached metadata, temporary files, WebView data, cookies, and Keychain credentials), confirms the wipe to the server, and returns to the login screen with an explanatory message. Photos already saved to the iOS Photos library are left untouched. (Issue #15)

## 1.0.0-rc.13

### Added
- **Download progress indicator**: Downloading an album now shows a persistent banner at the top with the current phase (downloading, extracting, saving to Photos), a progress bar with percentage and file counters, and a cancel button. The app stays usable while downloading, and tapping the banner opens a detailed modal view. (Issue #14)

### Fixed
- **Public album share links**: Links generated by the iOS app now match the server's URL style — they omit `index.php` when the Nextcloud server uses pretty URLs, so opening a shared album link in a browser now goes directly to the album with its photos instead of the timeline start screen. (Issue #7)

---

## 1.0.0-rc.12

### Added
- **Swipe-to-select**: Long-press a photo to enter selection mode, then swipe across multiple photos to select them in one gesture — like the iOS Photos app. Swiping over already-selected photos deselects them, and swiping near the screen edge auto-scrolls to extend the selection beyond the visible area. Implemented entirely on the iOS side (CSS `touch-action` + JS injection) without modifying the web app. (Issue #11)

### Fixed
- **Zoom**: Pinch-to-zoom no longer scales the whole app UI (photo grid, top bar and bottom navigation); the interface now always stays at its normal scale. (Issue #12)
- **Photo viewer**: A zoomed photo now fills the whole screen, including the areas behind the Dynamic Island / status bar, the home indicator and the side safe areas in landscape; black bars no longer remain at the screen edges. The WebView switches to full screen only while the photo viewer is open. (Issue #13)

---

## 1.0.0-rc.11

### Fixed
- **Sharing**: Public links for albums and photos now use the Nextcloud server address instead of the local address (127.0.0.1), so they work for anyone they are shared with. (Issue #7)
- **Setup**: The "Set up automatic upload" button now opens the Nextcloud app on the App Store instead of the Android app on Google Play. (Issue #8)
- **Device photos**: The "Upload Local" action is no longer shown when selecting photos stored only on the device, since uploading from the app is not supported yet. (Issue #9)

---

## 1.0.0-rc.10

### Changed
- **Album downloads**: Downloading an album now recreates it in Photos under the same name, without duplicating photos that already exist in the library.

### Fixed
- **Album sharing**: The "Share album" dialog now correctly shows the collaborator search field and public link options, and can be reopened after closing.
- **New album with collaborators**: The "Add collaborators" button now properly opens the full dialog when creating a new album.

## 1.0.0-rc.09

### Fixed
- **Downloads**: Downloading a photo or video no longer fails with "HTTP 403" error.
- **Save to Photos**: Downloaded photos and videos now go straight to the Photos app. On first use, the app explains why it needs full access to the photo library.
